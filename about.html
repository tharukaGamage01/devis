ABOUT US

This house is also one of the Nugapitiya Family's ancestral residences, also known as the Nuwara Walawwa, is an esteemed Sri Lankan family whose heritage is deeply woven into the cultural fabric of the island. Originating from the verdant highlands, the family is associated with a historic manor house that stands as a symbol of the island’s colonial past and traditional Sri Lankan architecture. The name “Nuwara” reflects their connection to the highlands (nuwara), where the family’s ancestral estate has been a center of local community life for generations.

Villa DEVI combines luxurious comforts with a minimalist approach, creating a uniquely peaceful setting that is free of noise and distractions, perfect for guests looking to unplug and rejuvenate. The attentive caretaker is available to provide unobtrusive service, ensuring every need is met while respecting guests’ privacy. Villa DEVI’s facilities cater to travelers seeking a truly tranquil, comfortable, and rejuvenating retreat.
